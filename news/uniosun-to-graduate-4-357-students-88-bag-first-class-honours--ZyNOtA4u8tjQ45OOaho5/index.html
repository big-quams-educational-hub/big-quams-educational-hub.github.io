UNIOSUN TO GRADUATE 4,357 STUDENTS, 88 BAG FIRST-CLASS HONOURS

20 September 2026· 1 min read

Osun State University (UNIOSUN) is set to confer degrees and diplomas on 4,357 graduands during its 16th Convocation Ceremony, with 88 students earning First-Class Honours.

The Vice-Chancellor, Professor Odunayo Clement Adebooye, disclosed this during a pre-convocation press conference held in Osogbo on Saturday, September 19, 2026.

Breakdown of Graduating Students

According to the Vice-Chancellor, the graduating class comprises:

  • 3,845 first-degree graduands.
  • 9 postgraduate diploma graduands.
  • 400 master's degree graduands.
  • 103 doctoral degree graduands.

Among the first-degree graduands, 88 students earned First-Class Honours, while 1,509 graduated with Second-Class Upper Division.

Another 1,946 students obtained Second-Class Lower Division, 220 earned Third-Class Honours, and three students graduated with Pass degrees.

UNIOSUN to Produce Its First Set of Medical Doctors

A major highlight of the 16th Convocation is the graduation of the university's pioneer set of 90 medical doctors from the Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) programme.

The Vice-Chancellor stated that the medical graduates were inducted by the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria (MDCN) on August 12, 2026, and are scheduled to receive their degrees on Wednesday, September 23.

University Highlights Academic Development

Professor Adebooye said the institution remains focused on strengthening academic programmes, research, professional partnerships, infrastructure and technology.

He also disclosed that students from 17 academic programmes introduced during his administration's first year would graduate at the convocation.

The 16th Convocation Ceremony is scheduled to run from September 18 to September 24, 2026.

The university's leadership has described the ceremony as an opportunity to celebrate academic achievement, resilience, innovation and character.
