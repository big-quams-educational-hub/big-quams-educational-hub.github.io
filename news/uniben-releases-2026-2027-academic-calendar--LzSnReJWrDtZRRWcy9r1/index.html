UNIBEN RELEASES 2026/2027 ACADEMIC CALENDAR

27 September 2026· 1 min read · 10 views
By Quwamdeen O. A.

The University of Benin (UNIBEN) has released its 2026/2027 academic calendar for full-time undergraduate programmes, with online clearance for newly admitted students scheduled to begin on Monday, October 5, 2026.

According to the calendar, fresh students are expected to move into the university’s halls of residence on Sunday, October 11, while returning students will resume in the halls on Sunday, October 18.

Lectures across all faculties and schools are scheduled to commence on Monday, October 19, 2026.

The university has also fixed Wednesday, November 11, 2026, for the matriculation ceremony.

KEY DATES

  • Online Clearance: October 5, 2026
  • Freshers’ Hall Resumption: October 11, 2026
  • Returning Students’ Hall Resumption: October 18, 2026
  • Lectures Begin: October 19, 2026
  • Matriculation: November 11, 2026

Students are advised to take note of the schedule and make the necessary preparations ahead of the new academic session.
