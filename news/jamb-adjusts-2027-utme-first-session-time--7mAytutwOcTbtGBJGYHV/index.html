JAMB ADJUSTS 2027 UTME FIRST SESSION TIME

14 September 2026· 3 min read

The Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) has adjusted the schedule for candidates taking part in the first session of the 2027 Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME), moving the required arrival time at Computer-Based Test (CBT) centres from 6:30am to 7:30am.

Under the revised arrangement, the first-session examination will begin at 8:30am and end at 10:30am.

The adjustment was made in response to safety and logistical concerns, particularly the difficulties faced by candidates who have to travel long distances to their assigned examination centres.

Previously, candidates scheduled for the first UTME session were expected to arrive at their designated CBT centres by 6:30am.

Candidates taking the first session of the 2027 UTME are expected to arrive at their centres by 7:30am, while the examination will commence at 8:30am.

The first session is scheduled to conclude at 10:30am.

The revised schedule is intended to reduce the pressure on candidates who would otherwise have to leave their homes in the very early hours of the morning to get to their examination centres on time.

JAMB said the decision was influenced by concerns about the safety and convenience of candidates, especially those travelling considerable distances to their assigned CBT centres.

For some candidates, an earlier 6:30am reporting time could require them to begin travelling before dawn.

The Board considered the issue during its 2026 Management and Information Technology Retreat, held in Ikot Ekpene, Akwa Ibom State, from September 6 to 11, 2026.

The retreat brought together JAMB management, state coordinators, heads of outstation offices and other stakeholders to review the Board's operations and preparations for the 2027 UTME.

Candidates scheduled for the first session should take note of the revised times:

ActivityRevised Time
Arrival at CBT centre7:30am
UTME begins8:30am
UTME ends10:30am

The change means candidates now have an additional hour to travel to their centres compared with the previous arrangement.

The adjustment specifically concerns the first session of the examination.

JAMB's other examination sessions will continue according to the Board's established scheduling arrangements unless further changes are announced.

Candidates should therefore avoid assuming that the 7:30am reporting time applies to every UTME session.

Candidates should carefully check their official JAMB examination details when the relevant examination information is released.

Those assigned to the first session should plan to arrive at their designated CBT centres by 7:30am and allow sufficient time for transportation and movement around their examination locations.

Candidates should also rely on official JAMB announcements for any subsequent changes to the 2027 UTME timetable, examination centres or other registration and examination requirements.

JAMB has already begun preparations for the 2027 UTME, with the Board using its management and technology discussions to address operational and logistical issues ahead of the examination.

The latest adjustment demonstrates the Board's consideration of the practical challenges candidates face when attending examinations at CBT centres across the country.

For candidates travelling from distant locations, the one-hour adjustment could provide additional time to reach their centres safely without having to begin their journeys as early as previously required.

The 2027 UTME first-session arrival time has been moved from 6:30am to 7:30am, while the examination will now run from 8:30am to 10:30am.

The change is aimed at addressing safety and logistical concerns, particularly for candidates who travel long distances to their assigned CBT centres.

Candidates should continue to monitor JAMB's official communications for the full 2027 UTME timetable and any further updates.
