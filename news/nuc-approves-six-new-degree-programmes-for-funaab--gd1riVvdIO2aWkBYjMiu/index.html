NUC APPROVES SIX NEW DEGREE PROGRAMMES FOR FUNAAB

24 June 2026· 1 min read · 0 views
By BIG QUAMS MEDIA

The National Universities Commission (NUC) has approved six new undergraduate programmes for the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB), effective from the 2026/2027 academic session.

The approval followed a successful resource assessment visit by the commission to evaluate the university's readiness to run the programmes.

The newly approved courses are B.Sc. Geophysics, Public Health, Biotechnology, Science Laboratory Technology, Water, Sanitation and Hygiene, and Clothing and Textile.

The development is expected to expand FUNAAB's academic offerings and further strengthen its commitment to producing skilled graduates in science, technology, health, and sustainable development.
