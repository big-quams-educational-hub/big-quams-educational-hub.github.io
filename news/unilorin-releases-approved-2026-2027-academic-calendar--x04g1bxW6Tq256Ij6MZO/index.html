UNILORIN RELEASES APPROVED 2026/2027 ACADEMIC CALENDAR

21 September 2026· 3 min read · 12 views
By Quwamdeen O. A.

The University of Ilorin (UNILORIN) has released its proposed academic calendar for the 2026/2027 academic session for undergraduate students.

The calendar, published in the Unilorin Bulletin dated Monday, September 21, 2026, outlines key academic activities, including registration, lectures, continuous assessments, examinations, semester breaks and result considerations.

KEY DATES FOR THE 2026/2027 ACADEMIC SESSION

  • Commencement of Academic Session: Monday, October 5, 2026.
  • Registration for New and Returning Students: Monday, October 5 – Friday, October 16, 2026.
  • Late Registration: Monday, October 19 – Friday, October 23, 2026.
  • Commencement of Harmattan Semester Lectures: Monday, October 19, 2026.
  • Matriculation: Friday, November 27, 2026.
  • Semester Break: December 24, 2026 – January 4, 2027.
  • Harmattan Semester Examinations: Monday, February 1 – Friday, February 26, 2027.
  • Mid-Session Break: Friday, February 26 – Sunday, March 14, 2027.
  • Commencement of Rain Semester Lectures: Monday, March 15, 2027.
  • Rain Semester Examinations: Monday, June 28 – Friday, July 23, 2027.
  • End of the Session Break: Begins Monday, September 6, 2027.
  • Commencement of the 2027/2028 Academic Session: Monday, October 5, 2027.
  • Resumption of New Students: Monday, October 5, 2027.

FULL BREAKDOWN OF THE PROPOSED ACADEMIC CALENDAR

1. Registration and Commencement of Lectures

The 2026/2027 academic session is scheduled to commence on Monday, October 5, 2026, with registration for both new and returning students running until Friday, October 16, 2026.

Late registration is scheduled for Monday, October 19 to Friday, October 23, 2026, while lectures for the Harmattan Semester are expected to begin on Monday, October 19, 2026.

Certificate clearance for the penultimate level is scheduled to take place from Tuesday, December 1 to Tuesday, December 22, 2026.

2. Harmattan Semester Activities

The university's proposed calendar provides for 15 weeks of instruction during the Harmattan Semester.

Other scheduled activities include:

  • Faculty Board consideration of the list of registered students: November 9–13, 2026.
  • Business Committee of Senate consideration of registered students: November 17–19, 2026.
  • Special Senate consideration of registered students: November 23, 2026.
  • Matriculation: November 27, 2026.
  • CBT and written continuous assessment: December 1–15, 2026.
  • End of Harmattan Semester lectures: Friday, January 22, 2027.
  • Lecture-free week and uploading of continuous assessment results: January 25–29, 2027.
  • Harmattan Semester examinations: February 1–26, 2027.

3. Rain Semester Activities

Following the mid-session break scheduled for February 26 to March 14, 2027, Rain Semester lectures are expected to commence on Monday, March 15, 2027.

The Rain Semester will include 15 weeks of instruction, continuous assessments and a four-week examination period.

Key dates include:

  • CBT and written continuous assessment: April 12–16, 2027.
  • End of Rain Semester lectures: Friday, June 18, 2027.
  • Lecture-free week and uploading of continuous assessment results: June 21–25, 2027.
  • Commencement of Rain Semester examinations: Monday, June 28, 2027.
  • End of Rain Semester examinations: Friday, July 23, 2027.

4. Final-Year Results and Goodstanding Considerations

The proposed calendar also contains dates for the consideration of final-year and goodstanding results by various university bodies.

Final-year result considerations are scheduled to take place between March and August 2027, while goodstanding results are scheduled for consideration in August 2027.

The Special Senate is scheduled to consider goodstanding results relating to the end of the 2025/2026 academic session on Monday, September 6, 2027.

5. End of Session and Next Academic Session

The end of the session break is scheduled to begin on Monday, September 6, 2027.

Teaching practice for students of the Faculty of Education is scheduled for September 15–October 31, 2027.

The 2027/2028 academic session is proposed to commence on Monday, October 5, 2027, which is also listed as the resumption date for new students.

IMPORTANT NOTE FOR STUDENTS

Students are advised to take note of the proposed dates and await further official communication from the university regarding implementation, adjustments or confirmation of the academic calendar.

The bulletin also states that Wednesdays, from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., are lecture-free for sports activities.
