LASU RETAINS WEST AFRICA’S TOP SPOT, CLIMBS TO 493RD GLOBALLY IN 2026 UI GREENMETRIC RANKING

25 September 2026· 2 min read · 1 view
By Quwamdeen O. A.

Lagos State University (LASU) has retained its position as the Overall Best University in West Africa in the 2026 UI GreenMetric Sustainable University Rankings, while recording a significant improvement in its global position.

The university disclosed this in a post on its official Facebook page, announcing that it placed 493rd globally among 2,016 institutions across 110 countries in the latest edition of the sustainability ranking.

According to the university, the result was announced at the UI GreenMetric Sustainable University Rankings 2026 Results and Awards Ceremony in Baku, Azerbaijan.

LASU’s 493rd global position represents a 267-place improvement from its 760th position in the 2025 ranking.

The university also said the latest result extends its record of leading universities in West Africa in the UI GreenMetric ranking since it joined the assessment in 2022.

LASU has reportedly maintained the top position in West Africa for five consecutive ranking cycles — 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026.

LASU Records Major Global Improvement

The university’s latest performance also reflects a significant improvement in its global sustainability ranking over the past three years.

LASU moved from 799th globally in 2024 to 760th in 2025, before advancing to 493rd in 2026.

This represents an improvement of 306 places since 2024 and 267 places compared with its 2025 position.

The UI GreenMetric World University Rankings assesses participating institutions based on their sustainability performance in areas including Setting and Infrastructure, Energy and Climate Change, Waste, Water, Transportation, Education, and Governance and Digitalisation.

LASU said its latest performance reflects its efforts to integrate sustainability, environmental responsibility, innovation and responsible practices into its operations.

University Gets Further International Recognition

The latest ranking comes shortly after LASU received additional recognition within the UI GreenMetric network.

The university said its National and Regional Coordinatorship was renewed in August 2026, alongside its elevation to Co-Chairmanship of key sessions at UI GreenMetric international events for the 2026/2027 academic session.

According to the Facebook post, the latest achievement reflects the collective contributions of the university’s management, staff, students, units and committees involved in advancing its sustainability agenda.

The university added that the recognition further strengthens its profile in areas such as sustainable development, environmental responsibility, innovation, governance and institutional transformation.

VC Congratulates LASU Community

The Vice-Chancellor of Lagos State University, Professor Ayodeji Olawunmi Badejo, congratulated members of the university community on the achievement.

He also commended staff, students and other stakeholders whose contributions have supported the university’s sustainability initiatives.

LASU said it would continue to build on the achievements of previous administrations while pursuing initiatives aimed at strengthening its position within the African and global higher education landscape.

The latest ranking adds another milestone to the university’s sustainability drive and its efforts to strengthen its global visibility.
