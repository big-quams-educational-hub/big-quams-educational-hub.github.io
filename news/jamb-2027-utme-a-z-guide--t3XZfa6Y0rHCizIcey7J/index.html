JAMB 2027 UTME: A–Z GUIDE

17 August 2026· 7 min read

As preparations begin for the next JAMB registration exercise, prospective candidates are advised to put their documents, personal information, course choices and other important details in order before generating their JAMB Profile Code.

This is important because some information supplied during the profile creation and registration process is linked to the candidate’s official records and may not be freely editable later. JAMB also advises candidates to ensure that their personal details correspond with their official documents before registration.

BEFORE YOU GENERATE YOUR JAMB PROFILE CODE, CHECK THESE:

A — AGE
Confirm that you meet JAMB’s age requirement for the admission year. Do not wait until registration before checking whether you are eligible.

B — BIO-DATA
Check your:

  • Full name
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • State of origin
  • Local Government Area
  • Nationality

Your information should be consistent with your official records, particularly your NIN.

C — CHOOSE YOUR COURSE CAREFULLY
Do not select a course simply because it is popular.
Research the course, career opportunities, O'Level requirements, UTME subject combination and admission requirements before making your final decision.

D — DOCUMENTS
Have your relevant documents and academic records available before visiting a CBT centre.
Candidates should know their O'Level results and grades where available. Candidates awaiting results should also understand the requirements for supplying the results when they become available.

E — EMAIL ADDRESS
Create and maintain access to a valid, functional email address that you personally control.
Do not use an email address you cannot access.

F — PHONE NUMBER / SIM
Your JAMB registration requires a unique mobile number.
JAMB states that the number used for registration becomes the candidate's unique identifier for transactions with the Board. No two candidates should use the same number. Keep the SIM active and accessible.

G — GENDER
Confirm that the gender on your official records is correct before registration.

H — HOME ADDRESS & OTHER PERSONAL DETAILS
Have your correct personal information ready. Avoid giving a CBT operator guessed or incorrect information.

I — INSTITUTION OF CHOICE
Research your preferred institutions before registration.
Know:

  • First choice
  • Second choice
  • Other choices, where applicable
  • Admission requirements
  • Whether the institution offers your preferred course

JAMB allows candidates to select different categories of tertiary institutions as choices.

J — JAMB PROFILE CODE
The Profile Code is generated using your NIN and unique phone number.
JAMB's official FAQ states that candidates generate the code by sending:
NIN [space] 11-digit NIN
to 55019 or 66019

NIN 01234567890

K — KNOW YOUR O'LEVEL REQUIREMENTS
Before selecting a course, confirm the required O'Level subjects and grades.
Do not assume that having five credits automatically makes you eligible for every course.

L — LOCAL GOVERNMENT & STATE OF ORIGIN
Confirm these details against your official records before proceeding.

M — NIN
Your NIN must be available and your personal information should be accurate.

Very important: Check your NIN details before generating your JAMB Profile Code. If there is an error in your NIN information, resolve it through the appropriate NIMC process before registration where necessary.

N — NAME
Check the spelling and order of your name carefully.
Do not generate your Profile Code casually if you already know that your official personal details contain an error.
JAMB's registration guidance stresses that candidates should ensure their NIN particulars agree with their supporting documents.

O — O'LEVEL RESULT
Know exactly what you have in WAEC, NECO or NABTEB and whether your result is available.
If your result is awaiting release, understand JAMB's requirements for awaiting-result candidates and ensure the result is uploaded when available.

P — PROFILE CODE
Keep your Profile Code safe once generated.
Do not share it carelessly with agents, friends or strangers.
JAMB advises candidates to keep security details confidential.

Q — QUALIFICATION
Candidates should understand the qualification required for their intended course and institution.
For Direct Entry candidates, additional information such as the previous institution, qualification, course and year of graduation may be required.

R — REGISTRATION CENTRE
JAMB registration is to be completed at JAMB-approved CBT centres.
Avoid registering through unapproved operators or allowing someone to conduct the entire process without your supervision.

S — SUBJECT COMBINATION
This is one of the most important things to determine before registration.
Check the correct UTME subject combination for your intended course.
A wrong subject combination can affect your eligibility for admission.

T — UTME/DE TYPE
Know whether you are registering for UTME or Direct Entry (DE).
DE candidates should have their relevant qualification details and supporting information ready before registration.

U — UNDERSTAND THE REGISTRATION PROCESS
Do not wait until you arrive at the CBT centre before learning how the process works.
Prepare your:

  • NIN
  • Unique phone number
  • Email address
  • Course choice
  • Institution choices
  • O'Level details
  • UTME subjects
  • Other required information

JAMB's official guidance requires candidates to provide their institution/course choices, email address, qualifications and UTME subjects during registration.

V — VERIFY EVERYTHING
Before you confirm your registration, carefully check every detail displayed on the screen.
Pay particular attention to:

  • Name
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • State/LGA
  • Course
  • Institution
  • UTME subjects
  • O'Level information
  • Email and phone details

JAMB specifically instructs candidates to cross-check their details before confirming registration with their biometric authentication.

W — WRONG INFORMATION
Do not allow anyone to enter information on your behalf without checking it.
A simple typing error can create serious problems later.
JAMB does provide procedures for certain corrections of data, but candidates should not register with the assumption that every detail can simply be changed later.

X — X-FACTOR: DO NOT RUSH
The pressure to "generate Profile Code quickly" should not make you careless.
Take time to put your records in order first.

Y — YOUR RESPONSIBILITY
Even if someone is assisting you with registration, you remain responsible for the information submitted on your profile.
JAMB states that candidates are liable for alterations made by others where they have shared their profile or security details.

Z — ZERO ASSUMPTIONS
Do not assume that:

  • Your course automatically accepts your UTME subjects.
  • Your O'Level result meets the requirements.
  • Your NIN details are correct.
  • Your institution is accepting your course.
  • Every piece of information can be edited later.

Verify before you generate your Profile Code and before you confirm your registration.

WHY YOU SHOULD PREPARE BEFORE GENERATING YOUR PROFILE CODE
Your JAMB Profile is not something to create casually.
The Board links the registration process to your NIN and unique phone number, and the profile remains important for present and future transactions with JAMB.
JAMB's official guidance also makes it clear that candidates should ensure their particulars on the NIN match their supporting documents and should carefully cross-check their registration details before final confirmation.
Therefore, before generating your Profile Code, make sure your NIN, name, date of birth, gender and other personal information are in order.

WHAT TO HAVE READY BEFORE GOING TO THE CBT CENTRE
Candidates should prepare the following:

  1. NIN
  2. Personal/unique active phone number
  3. Accessible email address
  4. Correct personal details
  5. O'Level result or awaiting-result information
  6. Preferred course
  7. Correct UTME subject combination
  8. Preferred institutions
  9. Relevant documents/qualifications
  10. Previous admission information, where applicable
  11. Any other information required by JAMB for the registration year

JAMB requires registration to be completed at approved CBT centres, and candidates are expected to personally present their Profile Code and e-PIN for registration.

IMPORTANT WARNING TO CANDIDATES
Do not generate your Profile Code using somebody else's phone number.
Do not use another person's NIN.
Do not allow an agent to choose your course or institution for you without your knowledge.
Do not confirm your registration without checking every detail.
Do not share your Profile Code, password, e-PIN or other security information unnecessarily.

JAMB advises candidates to keep their security details private and notes that registration is a personal process.

The best time to correct an error is before registration, not after.

Before generating your JAMB Profile Code, sit down with your documents and carefully verify your information. Research your preferred course and institution, confirm the required UTME subjects and O'Level requirements, and make sure your NIN details are accurate.

Once registration begins, do not rush because others are registering. Take your time, verify your details and only confirm the registration when you are satisfied that everything is correct.

For official updates, candidates should rely on JAMB's verified communication channels and official website rather than social media rumours.

JAMB Official Website — https://www.jamb.gov.ng

Prepare first. Verify twice. Register correctly.

Your JAMB registration is the beginning of your admission journey, make sure you start it right.
