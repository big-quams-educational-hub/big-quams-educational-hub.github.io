Rite Foods, JAMB Honour Seven Outstanding Undergraduates with ₦5 Million Academic Excellence Awards

1 July 2026· 1 min read · 1 view
B
By BIG QUAMS MEDIA

The Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB), in collaboration with Rite Foods Limited, has honoured seven exceptional Nigerian undergraduates with ₦5 million each under the maiden Academic Excellence Recognition Awards.

The initiative recognises candidates who demonstrated outstanding academic performance during the 2025 admission exercise, excelling in both the Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME) and their respective universities' Post-UTME or admission screening exercises.

According to JAMB, the beneficiaries emerged through a transparent, merit-based selection process. Six of the recipients represent Nigeria's six geopolitical zones, while the seventh award was presented to the highest-performing admitted candidate living with a disability.

The award-winning institutions are:

  • University of Lagos (South-West)
  • University of Uyo (South-South)
  • Federal University of Technology, Owerri (South-East)
  • Federal University of Applied Sciences, Kachia (North-West)
  • University of Maiduguri (North-East)
  • Federal University of Technology, Minna (North-Central)
  • University of Lagos (Candidate Living with Disability)

The University of Lagos emerged as the only institution with two award recipients in the maiden edition of the programme.

Speaking on the initiative, JAMB noted that the partnership with Rite Foods reflects a shared commitment to promoting academic excellence, rewarding merit, and encouraging Nigerian students to strive for outstanding educational achievement.

The ceremony attracted top stakeholders from the education sector, including the Honourable Minister of Education, JAMB Registrar, Prof. Is-haq Oloyede, vice-chancellors, heads of education regulatory agencies, corporate executives, and other distinguished guests.

The Academic Excellence Recognition Awards are expected to become an annual initiative aimed at celebrating exceptional academic performance and inspiring excellence among students across Nigeria.
