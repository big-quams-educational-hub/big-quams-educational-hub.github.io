UNIABUJA EXTENDS 2026/2027 POST-UTME REGISTRATION DEADLINE

3 September 2026· 1 min read · 1 view
By Quwamdeen O. A.

The University of Abuja (UNIABUJA) has extended the deadline for the 2026/2027 Post-UTME registration exercise.

The registration portal will now remain open until October 2026, giving prospective candidates more time to complete their applications.

Candidates who are yet to register are advised to take advantage of the extension and complete their Post-UTME registration before the new deadline.
