TASFUED Releases 2026/2027 Post-UTME and Direct Entry Admission Form

24 June 2026· 1 min read · 0 views
By BIG QUAMS MEDIA

The management of Tai Solarin Federal University of Education (TASFUED), Ijagun, Ogun State, has announced the commencement of its 2026/2027 Post-UTME and Direct Entry admission screening exercise.

Candidates who chose TASFUED as their first choice and scored at least 160 in the 2026 UTME are eligible to apply. Candidates who did not initially select the university can also apply after changing their institution on the JAMB portal.

The online application portal opened on Tuesday, June 23, 2026, and will close on Friday, July 10, 2026.

Interested applicants are advised to apply through the university portal at https://my.tasued.edu.ng and complete their registration before the deadline.
