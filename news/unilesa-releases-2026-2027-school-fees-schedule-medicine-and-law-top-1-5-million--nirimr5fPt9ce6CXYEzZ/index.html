UNILESA RELEASES 2026/2027 SCHOOL FEES SCHEDULE, MEDICINE AND LAW TOP ₦1.5 MILLION

19 September 2026· 2 min read

The University of Ilesa (UNILESA), Osun State, has released its school fees schedule for the 2026/2027 academic session, with tuition and other compulsory charges varying across faculties and programmes.

Students admitted into professional and specialised programmes, including Medicine, Law, Nursing, Radiography and Physiotherapy, are expected to pay higher fees than those in several other disciplines.

School Fees Breakdown by Faculty

  • Faculty of Arts: ₦372,000
  • Faculty of Education: ₦242,000
  • Management & Social Sciences (A): ₦397,500
  • Management & Social Sciences (B): ₦387,000
  • Tourism & Hospitality Management: ₦412,000
  • Faculty of Science: ₦432,000
  • Faculty of Computing: ₦432,000
  • BMS (A): ₦452,000
  • BMS (B): ₦842,000
  • Medical Laboratory Science: ₦842,000
  • Nursing Science: ₦1,312,000
  • Faculty of Law: ₦1,562,000
  • Faculty of Technology: ₦542,000
  • Faculty of Agriculture: ₦252,000
  • College of Medicine: ₦1,562,000

Professional Programmes Attract Higher Fees

According to the released schedule, students in the College of Medicine and Faculty of Law are expected to pay a total of ₦1,562,000, while Nursing Science students are required to pay ₦1,312,000.

Programmes under BMS (B), including Optometry, Physiotherapy and Radiography, have a total fee of ₦842,000.

The schedule also lists different tuition and acceptance fee amounts depending on the faculty and programme.

Other Compulsory Charges

In addition to tuition and acceptance fees, students are required to pay various charges, including:

  • Medical Fee — ₦30,000
  • Caution Payment — ₦10,000
  • Matriculation Fee — ₦10,000
  • ICT — ₦10,000
  • Library — ₦10,000
  • Sports — ₦20,000
  • Examination — ₦20,000
  • Entrepreneurial Training — ₦25,000
  • Wi-Fi — ₦13,000

Other charges, such as laboratory, bench and field-trip fees, may vary according to the programme and are reflected in the applicable fee totals.

Important Notice to Students

Prospective and newly admitted students are advised to consult the official UNILESA fee schedule and confirm the applicable charges for their specific programmes before making payments.

The figures reported are based on the published 2026/2027 fee schedule and should be verified through the university's official channels.
