Good to Know

How These Tools Work

1
GPA is Quality Points ÷ UnitsNot a plain average — each course's grade point is multiplied by its unit load first.
2
Classification bands are a default, not a universal lawFirst Class/2:1/2:2/Third-Class boundaries are set by each institution's Senate. What you see here is a common convention.
3
Aggregate formulas are school-specificWhere we have a school's actual screening formula on file, we show it and label it. Where we don't, we say so — we never guess and present it as fact.
4
Subject combinations are a general referenceAlways cross-check the exact institution/programme combination on JAMB's IBASS before you register.
5
Nothing here is stored on a serverSaved calculations live only in your browser's local storage — we don't collect or transmit them.
